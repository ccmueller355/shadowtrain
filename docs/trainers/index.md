## 🎨 Color Archetypes & Atelier Aesthetics

All editorial visual studies adhere strictly to the **Atelier Vael Cyber-Minimalist** aesthetic:

- **Matte Obsidian Canvas (`#14101C`)**: Ultra-deep studio void backdrop that eliminates ambient clutter and focuses 100% on anatomy and biomechanical form.
- **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**: HUD rings, telemetry lines, neural pulses, and precision optical guides.
- **Neon Amber (`#FF8C00`)**: Arcane micro-circuitry, torque alerts, and raw combustion indicators.
- **Emerald Pulse (`#39FF14`)**: Tactical telemetry, biological readiness, and military telemetry.
- **Phantom Violet (`#BF55EC`)**: Silent kinetic stealth, high-tension suspension, and aerial acrobatics.
